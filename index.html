Slingmoon: Orbit Hop

You are a small moon. Tap to let go of your planet, fly along your orbit, and get caught by the next one up before your orbit decays. One tap, endless sky.

  • Classic: climb as high as you can and chase the global leaderboard.
  • Daily Orbit: the same sky for every player, every day, with its own leaderboard.
  • Zen (Premium): no decay and no asteroids, just calm hopping.
  • Ice worlds, giant planets, crumbling planets and black holes that bend your path.
  • Power-ups: shield, slow-mo, shard magnet and extra time.
  • Collect shards for moon skins, trails and whole new worlds.
