How to play

Your moon circles a planet. Tap anywhere to launch it along the dotted line. If it passes through the dashed ring around another planet, it gets caught and starts orbiting that one. Every orbit decays: watch the ring around your planet and launch before it runs out. Skipping planets scores extra hops.

Planets and power-ups

Ice planets spin you faster. Giant planets are easier to land on but slower. Crumbling planets give you less time. Black holes pull your moon toward them as it flies past. Pick up a shield (survive one hit), slow-mo, a shard magnet, or extra time.

Purchases

Remove Ads turns off the ads between runs. Optional reward ads (continue a run, double shards) stay available. Premium unlocks Zen mode and every moon, trail and world, removes ads, and gives you a free continue each run and free shard doubling. Both are one-time purchases. Shard packs add shards to your balance. To restore Remove Ads or Premium on a new device, open the Shop and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Spent shards can't be restored. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Best scores, shards and unlocks are saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes them. Game Center keeps your leaderboard scores and achievements.
