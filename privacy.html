Privacy Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

Slingmoon is made by Santos & Halp Inc. ("we", "us"). You play without an account. We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not sell or share personal information.

What stays on your device

Scores, shards, unlocked items and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

Ads

Slingmoon shows ads supplied by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch (to continue a run or double your shards), and a short ad between some runs unless you bought Remove Ads or Premium. When an ad is requested, Google receives the information it needs to serve and measure that ad: your device's advertising identifier, device model and operating system version, coarse location derived from your IP address, and whether an ad was requested, shown or completed. We request non-personalised ads only, so Google does not use this information to build an advertising profile of you or target ads based on your interests. Google also uses it to limit how often you see the same ad and to detect invalid traffic. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's privacy and terms for business.

The app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account, or payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The game works without Game Center.

Crash reports

If you turned on Share with App Developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash and performance reports.

Your choices

You can reset or limit the advertising identifier in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and › Apple Advertising. Buying Remove Ads or Premium stops ads between runs.

Children

Slingmoon is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads are requested as non-personalised.

Your rights under U.S. state privacy laws

Residents of California (CCPA/CPRA) and other states with consumer privacy laws have rights to access, correct, delete, and port personal data, and to opt out of its sale, sharing, or use for targeted advertising. We do not keep personal information on our own systems, do not sell or share personal information, and do not engage in targeted advertising. Contact us with any request and we will respond within the time your state's law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
